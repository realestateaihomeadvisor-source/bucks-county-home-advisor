Should I sell to a "we buy houses" company in Bucks County?

It can make sense when speed or the home's condition matters more than price. A cash buyer can often close in 1 to 3 weeks with no repairs, showings or cleanout. But cash offers often come in well below market value.

The open market is still active. In September 2026, the median sale price in Bucks County was $515,000, and homes spent an average of 29 days on the market, according to the Bucks County Association of REALTORS (BCAR). Redfin reports a median of 36 days for the same month. Compare both paths before you choose (see the Bucks County housing market 2026).

How "We Buy Houses" Companies Work in Bucks County

Most of these companies follow the same four steps:

  1. You share your address and a few details by form or phone.
  2. They look at the house, in person or by video.
  3. They make a cash offer, often within 24 hours.
  4. You pick a closing date. Many say they pay the closing costs.

Most are investors. They plan to fix the house and resell it, or rent it out. Some are local Bucks County buyers. Others are national brands or wholesalers. A wholesaler signs a contract with you, then sells that contract to another investor for a fee.

How Cash Buyers Calculate Their Offer

Many investors use a formula called the 70% rule:

(Value after repairs × 70%) − repair costs = maximum offer

Value after repairs is what the house would sell for once it is fixed up. The 30% gap covers the investor's holding costs, selling costs and profit. Here is how that works on a Bucks County home:

Step Amount
Value after repairs $500,000
70% of that value $350,000
Minus estimated repairs −$40,000
Maximum cash offer $310,000

Not every buyer uses this exact formula. Some pay more for homes in good shape. But it shows why your home's value is the first number you need. Without it, you can't tell a fair offer from a low one.

When a Cash Sale Makes Sense

A cash buyer can be the right fit when:

  • The house needs major work, such as a roof, foundation, water damage, fire damage or mold.
  • You inherited the house and don't want to manage repairs and showings, especially from out of state.
  • You're going through a divorce and need a fast, clean sale.
  • You're behind on mortgage payments and facing a foreclosure date.
  • You're relocating for work on a short timeline.
  • The house has tenants who will stay after the sale.

If none of these fit and your home is in fair shape, listing it usually nets more in today's Bucks County market. See your options for selling a house in Bucks County.

7 Questions to Ask Any "We Buy Houses" Company

  1. Are you buying the house yourself, or will you assign the contract to someone else?
  2. How did you get your offer number? Can I see the comparable sales you used?
  3. Who pays the 2% transfer tax and the other closing costs? Is that in the contract?
  4. Can the price change after your inspection?
  5. How much is your earnest money deposit?
  6. Which title company will handle settlement?
  7. Can you show proof of funds?

Watch for these warning signs: pressure to sign the same day, a price cut right before closing, or any request for an upfront fee.

Check Your Township's Resale Rules First

Many Bucks County townships and boroughs require a resale use and occupancy (U&O) inspection before settlement. This applies to as-is sales too. Bensalem, for example, asks for the application one month before settlement, a $100 fee, and certificates for the electrical panel, heating system and chimney. Newtown Township does not require one for residential resales.

Rules differ by township, so call yours early. If the inspection finds problems, Pennsylvania's Act 133 lets a buyer accept a temporary certificate and fix them within 12 months. If a cash buyer says they will handle the U&O, get that in writing.

Frequently Asked Questions

How fast can a cash buyer close on a Bucks County house?

Often in 1 to 3 weeks. A title issue, an estate sale or a township U&O inspection can add time. Many townships want the U&O application at least 30 days before settlement.

Do I need an agent to sell to a cash buyer?

No. But a cash buyer works for themselves, not for you. Many sellers have a real estate attorney or agent read the agreement before signing.

Are "we buy houses" companies legit?

Many are. Check their reviews, ask for proof of funds, and make sure a licensed title company handles the settlement. Never pay a fee to receive an offer.

Will a cash buyer pay off my mortgage?

Yes. Your mortgage is paid off from the sale proceeds at settlement. You receive what is left after the payoff and any closing costs you agreed to pay.

Does my home's value matter if I'm selling as-is?

Yes. Cash offers are usually figured from your home's value after repairs. Knowing that number helps you see how big the discount really is.
