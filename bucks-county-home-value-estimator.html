How accurate are online home value estimators?

Where they work well

When comparable sales are plentiful and the home is similar to recent neighborhood sales.

Where they struggle

When the property is unique, data is thin, or condition and updates are hard to observe from public records alone.

What public tools miss

On-the-ground quality, micro-location appeal, and execution details that can move price outside a model range.

Bottom line:
Do not rely on one estimate for major decisions.
